Big Fish Support Guide

How to Get Help With Big Fish Games

Whether you are unable to sign in, download a game, install an update or launch a title, this guide provides general information to help you identify common problems and prepare for technical assistance.

01

Big Fish Games Support Overview

Big Fish Games players may encounter different types of problems while using games or account services. Issues can occur while signing in, downloading a game, installing software, updating a title or launching a game.

Identifying exactly when the problem occurs is one of the easiest ways to begin troubleshooting. Note what you were doing immediately before the issue appeared.

Helpful tip: Keep the game title, device information and exact error message available when seeking assistance.
02

Account & Login Help

Account problems may prevent access to games or other account features. Start by checking that the email address entered during sign-in is correct.

Things to check

  • Confirm that the account email is entered correctly.
  • Check that your internet connection is working.
  • Restart the application and try again.
  • Use the available account recovery process if necessary.
03

Password Recovery

If you cannot remember your account password, use the password recovery option provided on the account sign-in page.

Follow the recovery instructions carefully and make sure you are using the email address associated with the account.

Security reminder: Never share your password, verification codes or private account credentials with another person.
04

Big Fish Games Download Problems

Download problems can occur because of an unstable internet connection, insufficient storage, application issues or temporary service conditions.

01
Check your internet connection

Make sure other websites and services load normally.

02
Check available storage

Make sure enough free space is available for the game.

03
Restart the application

Close the application completely and try the download again.

05

Installation Issues

If a game downloads successfully but does not install, check your available storage and restart the computer or device.

Before trying again

  • Close unnecessary applications.
  • Check available disk space.
  • Restart the device.
  • Check for available software updates.
  • Record any installation error message.
06

Game Updates

Game updates can contain improvements, compatibility changes and fixes. Occasionally, an update may appear to stall or fail.

Check your internet connection and available storage before trying the update again. Restarting the application can also help with temporary problems.

Good practice: Avoid repeatedly interrupting an update while files are being installed.
07

Game Errors & Crashes

A game may sometimes freeze, crash or display an error message. Begin with simple checks before making major changes to the device.

Recommended first steps

  • Close and reopen the game.
  • Restart your computer or device.
  • Check for available game updates.
  • Check your internet connection when required.
  • Make sure sufficient storage is available.
  • Write down the exact error message.
08

Purchases & Billing

For purchase-related problems, identify the account and platform used for the transaction. Keeping the date and relevant transaction information available can make support conversations easier.

Privacy reminder: Never publish full payment-card numbers, passwords or security codes.
09

Refund Information

Refund procedures can depend on where a purchase was made. Purchases made through another app or distribution platform may be subject to that platform's applicable procedures.

For account-specific refund questions, check the current official support information for the applicable transaction.

10

Contacting Big Fish Games Support

If basic troubleshooting does not resolve your problem, use the appropriate official Big Fish Games support resources for account-specific assistance.

Information to prepare

  • Game title.
  • Device or operating system.
  • Short description of the problem.
  • Exact error message.
  • When the problem started.
  • Steps already attempted.
11

Big Fish Support Checklist

Before requesting assistance, make sure you have enough information to clearly explain what is happening.

01
Identify the problem

Explain exactly what happens and when it happens.

02
Record the error

Write down the exact error message whenever possible.

03
Try basic troubleshooting

Restart the application and device, then check the connection.

04
Prepare relevant information

Have your game and device details ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

Big Fish Games Support FAQ

Quick answers to common support questions.

Why is my Big Fish game not starting?
Restart the game and your device, check for updates and make sure sufficient storage is available. If an error appears, record its exact wording.
What should I do if I forgot my password?
Use the password recovery option on the account sign-in page and follow the instructions provided.
Why is my game download failing?
Check your internet connection and available storage. Restart the application and try the download again.
What information should I provide to support?
Provide the game name, device or operating system, description of the problem, exact error message and steps already attempted.
Can I get help with a purchase?
For account-specific billing or purchase questions, use the applicable official Big Fish Games support process.
Should I share my password with support?
No. Never share your password, payment-card details, security codes or other sensitive credentials.
